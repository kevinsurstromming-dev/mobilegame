# Mobilegame

A collection of 9 mini-games made in Unity. **Play it in your browser:** https://kevinsurstromming-dev.github.io/mobilegame/

1. Gravity Shift
2. Long Shot
3. Sky Squad
4. Sky Stack
5. Mole Mayhem
6. Shark Frenzy
7. Scrap Empire
8. Monster Island
9. Chaos City

Controls: touch on phones, mouse + WASD on PC.
